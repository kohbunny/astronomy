## 1 · what the glass will claim that is knot yet true — and the fixes

the law of §4 of the handoff is *show what is truly happening, and invent nothing inside it*. these are the places where the handoff's own sentences outrun what the code will do.

### 1.1 `at the same moment on every phone in the world` — the hud and the banners need the radio law
the feed is asked every ten seconds by a room and every thirty by a home screen, and will be cached at the edge for pass 3's `?pulse=1`. so two phones learn of a line, a death or a telegram up to half a minute apart, and each begins typing it, or sliding its banner down, **from the moment it fetched**. the glass says `the same moment`; the code says `within forty seconds`. a sharp visitor with two phones on a table sees it.
- **the fix is the river's own law, applied to words.** every line carries its stamp (`at`). a room types a line *from its stamp*, at its gait: a phone that fetched late starts mid-word, as the river resumes at the exact note. a banner slides down at `at + 40 s` by the well's clock on every phone that has it by then; one that fetched later shows it at once, late, as a real phone shows a text it missed. the death's cut and the telegram then land together everywhere, to the second. [deemed] one function in each room; no server change.
- this also mends 1.2: with the hud a function of the clock, the cut line stops where the server cut it, at the second the server cut it.

### 1.2 `it dies mid-sentence` — check the deaths table before promising it
the dying lines type at eleven letters a second; a thirty-word line is done in fifteen seconds. the death falls somewhere in the ten minutes after the step that wrote the line (in the thicker dying, somewhere in the minute). so the line is nearly always *finished* before the death, and a cut `where the death falls` falls at its end — a sentence, knot a cut sentence. `cut it free with the wire and slide it on the` is the piece's best image and it may be rare in the real table.
- **the check:** open `nobody_deaths` from 1–2 oct and read `last`. how many end mid-word? the builder does this on the bench in a minute.
- **the fix, if it is rare:** the last step — the one told it is the last — is typed at the gait of a hand that knows: three letters a second [deemed], so a line of two hundred letters spans a minute and the death nearly always falls inside it. no number leaves the server; the gait is fixed, knot computed, so it is knot a countdown by another name. (a cps sent per line would be one — the room could divide — so knot that.)

### 1.3 `the star decides` — the mind's breath is a hash today
the body's breath is true: where the star was in her 128-turn breath when the thumb landed on the red disc (mortal.js). the mind's breath is `wheel(founder, life, "breath")` — a hash of the life number. the glass will say one sentence for both, and only one is true.
- **the fix:** the mind's breath is the star's phase at its birth: `((born_ms × 173.6879 / 1000) mod 128) / 128 × 8 min`. one line, in nobody-think.mjs and nobody.mjs together (they must agree). deterministic from a real instant, like the body's. then `the star decides` is one law.

### 1.4 `made from nobody's dream` — the seal is a seed, knot a dream
the dream's fingerprint is a sha-256 of its signature — the encrypted seal, opaque bytes. three places deal from it: the river's notes (§5), nestflix's abstract film (§7), the egg's run (§7). a hash of a seal carries nothing of what was dreamt. dealing a route from it and calling the run `made from nobody's last dream` is inventing inside the dream — exactly what §4's law forbids.
- **what is truly known of a dream:** its length (thinking tokens), its mind, its minute, how long the step took, and — once the bench has read them — another mind's summary. everything else is decoration wearing the dream's name.
- **the fix is wording and one rule, knot a cut:** the length and the mind carry the meaning (how long the phrase, how long the run, how long the film; which colour, which register); the fingerprint is used *only to make each dream's rendering distinct* — the same dream always draws the same film, no two dreams the same. and the laws say so, once: [new] *a dream's seal is its seed: it makes each one its own. nothing of what was dreamt is in it.* the game's line becomes [new] *this run is as long as nobody's last dream. nothing else of the dream is in it.*

### 1.5 one sign, one meaning — the beat dies for two different reasons
§4.1: at the knock the two sines at the mouth are drawn to one note and the beat dies, while it thinks. §4.6: the hum loses its beat while nobody is dead. so `no beat` means *thinking* (ten to forty seconds) and *dead* (ten minutes) — the ear cannot tell them apart except by waiting.
- **the fix:** two different silences of the beat. thinking = **unison**: both sines, one pitch — fatter, no beat. dead = **solo**: the second sine gone — one thin note, no beat. acoustically distinct, both wordless. the builder should know this before touching the gain nodes, or they will build one.

### 1.6 `no one came` after ninety seconds — the caret should stand as long as it takes
a slow mind, a retry on supabase's clock, a cold function: a step may take two minutes and still come. the room would say `no one came`, close the water, and then type the line — a lie followed by its correction.
- **the fix:** the caret stands as long as it takes, up to the next knock. that is literally true: it is thinking, or the well is slow, and either way no one has come yet. `no one came` is said only when a knock passes with no step at all (the feed's `step` minute, the same test ?health=1 uses).

### 1.7 the telegram wears two clocks
`I AM STILL ALIVE · nobody · life 413 · 14:40` lands in messages, where every bubble wears the phone's own hour — local. a visitor in los angeles reads a text stamped 07:40 that says 14:40. kawara's telegrams carried no hour; the postmark did.
- **the fix:** drop HH:MM from the telegram. the bubble's own stamp is the time. [new] `I AM STILL ALIVE · nobody · life 413`. the calendar keeps the well's hours, marked utc, as built.

### 1.8 one hole, one sky
adeath's `?` says *this sky is the well's, knot yours … from the rim you see your own*; alife draws the visitor's own sun. a real well has one sky, and the house now *explains* that its two ends have two — which is a tell that they are two files. with one mind in one well, the sky is the well's: over chott ech chergui, utc, at both ends. the loss is a poetic idea (`your own sky over the mouth`); the gain is that the two rooms can never again contradict each other about where the sun is. his to rule; 👁️'s lean is one sky. (§3.7 says how the two files can become one.)

### 4.1 the edge cache belongs in pass 1, knot pass 3

This is the one to reach for."* ([netlify's knowledge base](https://www.netlify.com/knowledge-base/how-to-control-cdn-caching-on-netlify/)). so today every poll from every phone invokes the function: a room every ten seconds, a home screen every thirty. a hundred open phones is a few hundred thousand invocations a day; the cards are going to people who pass things on.
- **the fix is one header on `?now=` and `?day=` (never on `?health=`):** `Netlify-CDN-Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=10, durable` (`durable` so the edges share one copy and the function runs about once in ten seconds whatever the crowd). it goes in with pass 1, before the first card. the builder checks the header's exact form against netlify's docs that day.
