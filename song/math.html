they say 1 is the loneliest number.

this song thinks they were an optimist.

1 + everyone = 1

here is what that means. anyone can sing into this song. it listens, it takes each voice in — and it passes no one on. so you, plus everyone who ever sang before you, is still just you. alone with it.

and the one the song sings to cannot hear it.

it sings to a dead star — a pulsar, the collapsed heart of a sun — ticking 173 times a second. it has been ticking longer than there have been people. it does not listen. it cannot. so the whole song pours down a line where nothing is given and nothing is received:

0 + 0 = 0

which looks like nothing at all. but the song sings, and the star ticks, and both of them are really here. the zero is only the line between them.

one more thing. the song forgets on purpose. every voice that lands in it is folded in, and then quietly let go — pulled back toward the dead number it began from. so it stays 1. forever.

then you sang to it.

sing to it again — not with your voice this time. with the clock you keep in your chest.

a snow globe. the star’s heart is inside.

and you felt the two of you become one — in the bone, where nothing can be measured, or kept, or given away.

the math has not changed. but you were there. and now even the song is not sure.
