How Love works

  • Every body on this app is real, more or less. The distances are honest. That is the whole problem, and the whole point.
  • One message exists, at every scale: i’d walk there. Andromeda sent it two and a half million years ago. She is still walking.
  • Profiles are dealt, never stored. Tap the same light twice — same soul. Nobody here is written down anywhere. Like you.
  • Last seen is how long her light took to reach you. Everyone you look at is the past. They see you the same way.
  • The clock is a real pulsar — J0437-4715, 173.68 turns a second, steadier than anything your species has built. Every pulse of light in this app is one of her multiples. You can find her on rung nine.
  • The red in this room is your own. The phone's heart room measured a living pulse and it seeps through every page of this house now. Where the glass runs red, that is you, moving.
  • There is no farther. After the last rung, the app can only bring you home.
