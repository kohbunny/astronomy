about 512 light years away, a dead star is spinning. it turns roughly 173 times every second, steady as a clock, and it has kept that time since long before anything on earth could listen. i was made from its turning — the low tone you are about to meet.

you will hum, and slide your voice until your hum and i become a single sound — not in the air, but inside the bone of your skull. when we meet, the small wobble between us goes still. it is real: your head is truly ringing at the star's rate. and it is yours only — no one beside you can feel me, and you cannot hand me to them. for a little while, you are the only one inside a sound from the far edge of the sky. that is the whole of it. it is worth trying once.

practice — the target is hidden. press, hold, and drift until the wobble stops.

one sound

the real thing happens against your skull, in the next room. this is only practice.

there are two ways to meet me. the first takes a minute, in your hand. the second is the whole thing, lying down. for both, close your eyes.

in the hand

hold me in your hand like a shell you are listening to. turn my screen to face away from you. press my speaker end gently into your ear — at the little flap of cartilage in front of the ear-hole, where it meets your jaw.

touch me once; i will rise. now hum, and move your voice up or down by tiny amounts. listen for the moment i stop sounding like a note and turn into a dry buzz, like static held against your head. that buzz is me crossing into bone. you may feel me in your skull first, then lower, in your chest. stay there. eyes closed.

lying down

this one you set up before it begins, so that once it starts you do nothing but listen.

lie on the floor. rest me on the ground with my speaker end by your ear, and prop my far end against something — a wall, a book, a cup, a pillow — so i lean back at a low angle, propped, not flat, and cannot slip. settle your head so my speaker end meets the same spot as before, the flap in front of the ear. get comfortable.

then — and only then — reach up and tap me to start, and let your hand fall. hum, and find the one sound exactly as before: your voice sliding until i become a buzz and the wobble stills. eyes closed. lie still.

there is no clock here, and no right way. when your voice and i become one sound, you have arrived — there is nothing after it to reach, nothing to find or finish. stay as long as you like. to leave, simply take me from your ear. i hold for exactly as long as you do.
