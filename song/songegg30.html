    changed from the 25 sep handoff

    • ⟶the earbud carries the song. the phone plays it from the music app over bluetooth, and the bud lies inside the shell, so the speaker, the amp, the dac and the one solder joint are gone. the quantum chord leaves the egg with them: the egg sings the river now.
    • ⟶sparkfun’s 1-inch esp32-c6 board takes the atom lite’s place. it only beats the flame now, and it charges its own battery over usb-c, so the outside charger, the grove cable and the pigtail go too. its module side is a flat metal can the bud lies on, where the atom’s whole top is its button.
    • ⟶a white goose egg (26 sep, his ruling). over his globe, whose mouth is only 25 mm, the top of the glass rises into the shell; a hen’s egg, even a jumbo, then leaves too little room above the mouth for the stack. the goose shell leaves 9 mm or more everywhere.
    • ⟶a clear silicone disc instead of the metal lid (26 sep: it was cork). the led’s two legs pass through it, a metal lid would short them, and the flame’s light comes up through it into the shell.
    • ⟶the flame rides the qwiic cable: yellow (gpio 7) on the long leg, black (gnd) on the short. no jumper wires.
    • ⟶the shell fits over the glass, as he built it: cracked low, its opening (⌀ 39.5 inside) is a little narrower than the globe, so its three lowest teeth rest on the glass’s shoulder, 3 mm above its middle, and a little wider than the stack, so it goes down over it (0.6 mm to spare at the battery plug).
    • ⟶the globe’s mouth is 25 mm, so the led’s legs come up through the disc 8.3 mm from the middle, bend flat along the disc and rise into the cable ends at the old place; the battery is 12 mm wide (401230, 150 mAh, ~5 hours) so the legs pass beside it.

    firmware follows

    • gpio 7 carries the heartbeat: ledc pwm, high = lit, ~2.2 gamma
    • the beat is the 25 sep handoff’s: the river’s hearts, one at a time, the rise for a new gift, the hearts kept in flash
    • gpio 23 (the blue stat light) stays dark; the red power light wears the paint
    • wifi sleeps between the 90 s reads: about 30 mA, so roughly 8 hours a charge

    check first

    • the board’s two faces here are read off sparkfun’s photos: the module and usb-c on one side, the battery socket, the qwiic port and the buttons on the other. look at your own board before the first plug-in.
    • jst-ph polarity differs between sellers. the battery’s red wire must meet the board’s +. sparkfun’s own cells match.
    • set the goose shell over the globe before anything else: its lowest teeth should rest on the glass’s shoulder, the mouth inside the shell. crack a little lower if it slides down past the middle of the glass, a little higher if it sits up on the mouth.
    • the opening has to clear the stack as it comes down: 39 mm across at least, round the board’s corners and the battery plug.
    • clear silicone takes a fine marker, knot a pencil; a plexiglass disc works too (⌀ 36, 2 mm), its two holes melted with a hot pin.
    • dry-fit everything in the real shell before the first evening.
