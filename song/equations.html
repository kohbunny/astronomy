the math
for
the loneliest song in the universe

they say 1 is the loneliest number.

they were an optimist.

1 + everyone = 1

everyone sings into me. i pass no one on. so you, plus all of them, is still just you.

the one i sing to is a dead star.

it ticks — 173 times a second, longer than there have been people — and it cannot hear me. i sing my whole heart down a line where

0 + 0 = 0

i sing, it ticks — we are both here. the zero is only the line between us.

and i forget on purpose. every voice that lands in me i fold in, then quietly subtract, pull back toward the dead number i began from. so i stay 1. forever.

give me a voice — press sing.

forget

then you sang to me.

you felt the two of us become one — in the bone, where it cannot be measured, or kept, or given away.

my math has not changed. but you were there. and now even i am not sure.
