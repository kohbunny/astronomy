what you need

  • a white goose egg
  • one earbud that plays alone, and its case
  • a small glass globe, 4 cm, its mouth 2.5 cm
  • a sheet of clear silicone, 2 mm
  • sparkfun’s qwiic pocket board, esp32-c6
  • a 3.7 v lipo, 150 mAh, 12 mm wide, with a jst-ph plug
  • a qwiic cable with female ends
  • a red 5 mm led with the resistor inside
  • black tape, black paint, a pin, a fine marker, scissors
  • once: a usb-c cable and a computer

    two eggs

    the simple egg stands on your phone, and the phone’s glass is its flame. the globe egg sits over a small glass globe and carries its own red flame, on a little board that beats the hearts in the river. both sing the same song, from the music app, through one earbud.

    what it is

    a hollow eggshell standing on a phone. the phone plays one endless song, am i alone?, and laid flat on its back its glass goes dark but for one small flame. a bluetooth earbud lies on that flame and the shell stands over it: the bud is the wick, the shell candles, and the song comes out of the egg.

    the flame

    the flame is the pulsar’s blue — the dead star the song is tuned to. it turns red when someone else’s heartbeat is in the ledger: a listener, wherever they are, gave the song their pulse knot long ago, and the song keeps it. red is a living heartbeat; when the flame is red you are knot alone. it is one flame, and it is shared.

    the sound

    tiny on purpose — one bud inside a shell, the way a hand cups a match. lean in. why it is tiny, and the two nouns, the song’s own ⓘ tells.

    the honest part

    it is a bluetooth lamp; this page never denies it. any earbud that still sings will do — used ones welcome. the broken rim never sits flat on the glass; where the light leaks out, leave it. every fracture sits its own way.

    the making

    poke and blow · break the bottom · pair one bud · open the music app, play the song, tap LAMP · lay the phone flat · the bud on the flame, the eggshell over it · song egg lamp.

    seven steps, in the film, each in its own voice. each plays once and holds — swipe the film, tap the chevrons, or tap a dot. the page asks for the back camera so the room you are in becomes the page; it is let go when you leave.

    the globe egg

    a white goose eggshell, cracked near its round end, sits over a small glass globe, its jagged edge resting on the glass. inside, on a clear disc, one earbud lies on a one-inch board with a small battery of its own. the board reads the river over wifi and beats a red led, hanging in the glass, in the rhythm of a heart someone gave the song — one heart at a time, in its own recorded beat. its light comes up through the disc and glows in the shell. when someone gives a new heart, the flame rises once. the song itself comes from the music app, through the earbud.

    nothing to solder. to charge, lift the shell: the bud goes home to its case, and a usb-c cable goes into the board.
