card probe

a contact card can show a name and hide an address behind it — maybe. each row of this card tries one way. a row that works opens this page again, and the page says which row it was and whether the genes came through the #.

what to do

  1. tap the button. in the sheet, send the card to yourself — Messages to your own number, Mail, or Notes all work.
  2. open the card where it landed. look at what each row prints, then tap every row that will take a tap.
  3. a tap that lands here shows the row's letter. a tap that does nothing, or opens something else, is also an answer.
  4. then create new contact, open it from Contacts, and tap the rows once more — the preview and the saved contact do knot always agree.
  5. tell 👁️ what each row printed and what each tap did. screenshots of the card are perfect.

the rows

if Contacts refuses the whole card, or a row is missing from it, try the rows one at a time — each of these raises the sheet with a card carrying that one row.
