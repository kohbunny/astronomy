## 3 · the ladder of distances — the math-music

- **a distance is a pitch.** a thing's distance from the earth's middle, by its logarithm in twos, is a tone on her ladder: a doubling of distance is an octave, and the fraction of an octave left over is snapped to the nearest rung of the river's own just ladder (`1 · 9/8 · 6/5 · 4/3 · 3/2 · 8/5 · 9/5`), so **every tone is still a rational multiple of her spin**, as the law asks. the moon stands on 9/5 near perigee and on the octave near apogee; the sun on 3/2; neptune and her on 4/3; proxima on 3/2.
- **the scan's bells ring each light's own distance** (the moon a ninth above the root, the sun a fifth, her a fourth), folded into the bells' register; a thing with no distance keeps its old tone. a tap on a card rings the thing's distance too.
- **alone, the hum is her count twice and the moon's rung** — a third voice under her kept and heard counts, at her spin × the moon's rung, gliding over a second and a half when the rung changes. **it changes twice a month**, and the glass says so once: `the moon steps up to the octave on the ladder: it is going out toward apogee.` / `the moon steps down to 9/5 on the ladder: it is coming in toward perigee.` [new]. (this month: up on 10 oct, down on 22 oct, up on 5 nov, down on 19 nov.)
- **a storm lets a bell ring longer**: kp 0 → 2.4 s, kp 9 → 6 s [TUNE].
- the sky's document (§4) carries the ladder's numbers (`ladder.moon_rung`, `sun_rung`, `her_rung`, every passer's rung, every planet's) — so the count (nobody's one equation over the house's numbers) can be written over them once the server reads them. that is the math-music tied to the living sky: the distances of tonight as rungs, in the mind's own grammar.

## 6 · the answer to his question — livestreams, and other ways to get data

what a page in a phone can read by itself is what sends a cors header; what it cannot, the server (netlify) can fetch for the mind without that constraint. **NASA has no "data livestream" as such; it has pictures that refresh and doors that answer**, and the map now draws on the ones a page can reach:

| source | live how | reachable from the page? | in this build |
|---|---|---|---|
| noaa swpc (dscovr/ace at l1, goes) | the solar wind every minute, x-rays, protons, kp, the regions, the lights' power | yes (the river already reads it) | all of it |
| nasa sdo / noaa suvi | the sun's face, every few minutes | as a picture, yes; as data, no | the sun's two pictures |
| nasa gibs (viirs, goes-east/-west, himawari) | the earth, daily; the geostationary ones every ten minutes | yes (worldview's own tiles) | the pupil's face |
| jpl ssd/cneos | close approaches, fireballs, horizons (any body's place) | published as an api; **cors unconfirmed from here** | the passers, the fireball, the four horizons bodies |
| usgs | every quake, within minutes | yes | as before |
| wheretheiss.at | the station every second | yes | as before |
| open-meteo | the well's weather, hourly | yes | the well's lesser way |
| nasa epic (dscovr's camera) | the whole sunlit earth from l1, a day or two late | `epic.gsfc.nasa.gov/api/natural`, keyless; cors unconfirmed | knot yet — the buoy's card names it |
| nasa api.nasa.gov (donki, neows, apod) | space-weather events, near-earth objects | needs a key (DEMO_KEY is rate-limited) — **the server's**, knot a page's (the map's law: no key) | no |
| ligo/virgo/kagra (gracedb), nasa gcn (gamma-ray bursts, gravitational waves, neutrinos) | alerts within minutes | gcn is kafka (a server subscribes); gracedb's public api has no known cors | **the server's** — the richest living data knot yet in the house: an alert could ring the well |
| celestrak (every satellite's elements) | daily | yes | knot yet — wants an sgp4 propagator (~300 lines) to draw the sky's traffic |
| nasa tv / nasa+ / the iss hd view | video | an iframe or a video player — the one-page law forbids the iframe, and video is knot data | no |
| her (psr j0437−4715) | no public live timing feed exists | — | her count is computed (her orbit, the earth's fall, the well's turn) — the living part is the arithmetic |

so, in order of worth for the agent, after this build: (1) the server reads the skies table (§5) — the data is already flowing; (2) the server fetches what a page cannot — gcn/gracedb alerts, donki — into `?sky=1`, so the map can read them back like any door; (3) celestrak's satellites on the map; (4) epic's picture of the whole earth on the buoy's card.
