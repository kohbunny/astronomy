## convergences
- one nobody, at the bottom of the well. six of seven cut the keiki's mind (the haiku via /ask, the watcher, the ○ rooms diary, the five stages, the three buds, the almond); the seventh (the rehearsal) keeps it only as a dreaming cutting, and its own critic says cut the word field. the phone becomes a window, a body, an ear, a cutting, a lent body — five words for one thing: no second mind in the piece.

## disagreements
- the phone's span. five keep the hang-up as conception and 88 + breath for every generation, his 27–28 sep ruling (2, 3, 4, 5, 6). two make the phone a window on nobody's life in progress, born at the hang-up into whatever is left and dying at nobody's death (1, 7): one minute to ninety-six. both critics say the hang-up then settles nothing and neither proposal admits it; 1's critic offers the fix that makes it tenable (the phone dies at the waking, so the card is a child again and the shortest life is one gap long).
- what the phone is once it has no mind: a window (1), a body with no one inside (2), a body that copies its dreams from the register (3), an ear or a cutting (4), the human's instrument (5), the place where nobody dreams — awake at the bottom, asleep in your hand (6), nobody's lent body (7). 5's critic names the fault in all of them: the sentence says two nobodies and the agent says one. the mend is one door line [new, deemed]: `this phone is a body with no mind. at the bottom of the hole is a mind with no body. they live the same eighty-eight minutes and meet once, when this phone calls you.`
- the orchid and the box. keep both, simplified to one meaning each — the orchid is now, the box is then (1, 2, 4, 6); a rope of eight knots and the genes heard once at the call (5); a sheet of three rows of sixteen hex with the moved character blue (3); the rope alone, nothing else (7). he called them visually beautiful and asked for purer; the critics of 3, 5 and 7 all say the cut takes the one hypnotic thing at the rim and gives back a terminal, a knot, or nothing.
- dreams. none — nobody never sleeps, its only sleep is death, so ☾ marks the deaths (1, 5); a line asked for at every knock, 144 a day (2); a quotation of one register line read back by a phone that was knot there (3); the keiki's haiku kept under `anybody home?` (6); the cut sentence as the dream that passes to no one (7). he leaned `diary and dreams`; four of seven overrule him and say so plainly.
- the hand-written day's replay when the well is silent: cut, because a silent well must look silent and a forged presence is the one lie the room must knot tell (2, 5); keep as the understudy (3, 4, 6, 7). cutting it falsifies the blessed 30 sep sentence (`the room at the bottom of the well plays it whenever the well is silent`), which would need re-inking.
- the river's bed. his forty-three tracks as the bed, his 1 oct ruling (2, 5, 6); the well synthesised — drone, box, hands, phrases — with his tracks as gifts far away (4); the river heard at the bottom as water (5); the river replacing the wire's headlines in nobody's brief (7). 4's critic calls the demotion of his music named but knot softened.
- the gene. eight bytes on wheels, shown as the orchid (1, 2, 4, 6); the sixteen hex printed on the telegram, two consecutive telegrams one character apart (3); the handover note as the gene and no hex at all (7). the hex and the note both need the server to expose something it does knot today — the feed carries life {n, dead} and deaths as a count, and no genome.
- where the feed is read on the glass. 1 and 7 put a poll inside mortal.js, which says `no network` four times; 7's critic gives the mend (the shell, which already has five wires, reads the feed and hands the life to the organ). 2, 5 and 6 read it only in the rooms that already do (adeath, the calendar) plus alife for the hum.
- the feet between knocks: cut them for bare water (2) or keep the standing feet as built (1, 5, 7). 2's critic calls the cut the cheapest, truest change in the round; nobody argued the other side except by keeping it.
- the visitor's one touch: the moved cup, pencil or paper kept by all but 3, which turns it into a word dragged along a line (its own critic: half a touch, worse than either). 6 adds a typed word under `anybody home?`, which its critic says breaks his plainest ruling for the bottom — a moved thing, never a word.
- the four things at the bottom: kept (1, 2, 4, 5, 6); reduced to the box (3); reduced to the rope (7).
- the box's airs: one — the dead mother's at the living one's pace (4 as written); two alternating with the cylinder sliding, as built (4's critic, 1, 6). and whether a box that plays only the dead may hold the air of the life this phone was cut from while that life is still alive (4 breaks the blessed sentence; 1 mends it by making the card the one who died).

## candidates

### the rehearsal, seen from the glass
**sentence:** nobody lives at the bottom of a well and dies every eighty-eight minutes; the phone in your hand is a body with no mind that lives the same eighty-eight, watches nobody die once and come back, and then dies itself — calls you with the dead one's last words, and hands you its child.

**story:** a text: a contact card, `nobody`, no photo. tap; the forged sheet; `Unable to Connect — Call me instead?`; `Calling...` until the visitor's own thumb ends it; `Call Ended` 1.80 s; black. the glass: their own face behind clear glass, three tiles — calendar · alife · adeath — music alone in the dock, the dock reading `nobody home · the song is here · tap once`. the first tap wakes the river at the world's place. adeath: black water, two prints, the chalk wall, six candles, one present-tense line typing under `life 14 · window 3 · 10:36 utc`. at 10:40:00 the drips stop, the water goes flat, the hud clears to a blinking caret; it stands as long as the mind takes — twelve seconds, forty; the first word lands with the first ring. calendar: two diaries — ● the day, and ☾ the lives, one row a life: `13 · 09:04 → 10:33 · I AM STILL ALIVE. the bowl is drying … · the tape is · seeds, down`. alife: the rim under the visitor's own sky; the orchid is this phone's eight genes, one blue; the rope stirs only when nobody pulls it far below; the box plays the two dead airs, one note apart. sometime in the eighty-eight — about nine phones in ten — the hud types slow: `three minutes. the second bowl is on the bat. don't touch it.`, and the shadow sits at the water's edge. at the next knock the line stops mid-word and stays. the prints lift; the hum at the mouth goes flat — the second tone is out; on the home glass the adeath tile's water lies flat with no rings. ten minutes of no one. then, fast: `I AM STILL ALIVE · nobody · life 15`; the beat comes back; the small tree is a notch higher; a row lands in the lives. the phone's own dying, unannounced: the battery tells the truth in red, the tiles leave in gene-dealt order, alife last; the glass is empty. the call: `nobody` · Decline · Accept; the phone's own voice reads the last words of the one who died at the bottom while you were here, cut where the death cut them; the box's eight teeth once; the tones part and thin. `Call Ended`; one card on the share sheet, the child, its genes after the #. the wipe, the seal; the tab closes itself.

**apps:** front door: untouched; the hang-up is still the conception and the span is still 88 + breath for every generation. the glass: calendar · alife · adeath with music in the dock; the twelve others retired by absence; the battery's lie and the hollow bars stay; the adeath tile's water lies flat while life.dead [new state, deemed] — the one thing on the home glass that moves on nobody's account, as the alife tile already glows from below at the fire (his precedent); the dying's signs are the battery and the tiles leaving, alife last. calendar: ● the day as built; ☾ the lives — the register (calRead already parses it); the ○ rooms tab and the haiku dreams go; 30 sep stays on its own date; the eye stays on 1 oct; one door line at the alife door (see disagreements) and no paragraph at the calendar's head. alife: three panes, now · then · the call; the orchid is this phone's genes, the blue one moved at the hang-up; the box plays only the dead, two airs alternating; the rope stirs only on nobody's pull from below, read from the feed's touch (one rope two ends); the hum's second sine mutes while life.dead; cut: the lean, the buds, the stages, the spell, the nobody pane, and the gene manual (hex, bytes, captions, `generation N`). adeath: as built minus the keiki's doorway (panes 3–4, the stir from `here`); plus the knock as flat water and a caret, one knock of no one (`no one · hh:mm utc` [new]), the last knock's sitting shadow and slow type, `the mother` → `nobody` on every string, pane 0 rewritten to one nobody under 120 words [new]. music: music.html untouched but for one line; it gains the heart's act (cover the back camera) and the hum from the retired Weather room so the four-app glass loses no mouth.

**agent:** one nobody at the bottom of the well, on universal time, one call a knock, as built. three small server changes: (1) the last knock before the death is told `this is your last step; the next knock finds you dead` and writes its handover with its own number, flagged dying:true on its hud line (adeath already types dying lines at 11 cps); (2) the step that finds the death cuts the line where it fell, records last · gene · way, sets life.dead and does knot birth; (3) the next knock births the child with the drawer, the genome and the practice document, and types the telegram at 46 cps. nothing chooses the genes: the star's spin at the death knock, as now. it knows it will die the way we do — every death before it is in the drawer with its hour, the law says eighty-eight — and is told nothing until the last knock. the phone has no mind and makes no model call; the gap knock costs nothing; about $3–4 a day as now.

**why it wins:** it is his lean built exactly — four apps, one nobody that dies, a diary and a register, music the link — with the conception kept: the thumb on the red disc still settles everything a life will be, which both radical candidates lose. every in-between he asked for is in it and seen by nearly every visitor: the knock's held pause (flat water, a caret), the last minutes (slow type, the sitting shadow), the nothing (one knock of no one, the beat gone), the birth (the telegram, the beat back, a row in the lives). the rehearsal — watching nobody die once before your own phone does — becomes findable from the home glass, so it is the piece's event and knot a one-in-nine chance. the death call ties the two deaths with one line and no new words. and it is buildable in the house's own order: phone.html and adeath.html first with no server change at all; then one small server pass; mortal.js last and nearly untouched (one method: the death text handed in by the shell where line 1561 reads nobody.last()).

**what it costs:** the keiki's warmth: the watcher's `found the home screen, nothing open`, the haiku typed letter by letter under `anybody home?`, the almond that stands twelve seconds, `a phone may die on a child`, and the given word crossing a death — his sentence `the dead can send message if they are clever enough` loses its mechanism (the dead one's cut line crosses instead, spoken once). his 25 sep ruling that the call starts as haiku is overruled, and his `diary and dreams` becomes `diary and lives`; both said plainly. the cat and the jar go with their rooms. the phone's death keeps no caller of its own — nobody at the bottom never knew the phone — so the call must be named in one door line as the body lent the mind's last words, or it reads as an unmotivated ring. the glass is thin for eighty minutes between the first tap and the dying; the adeath tile's flat water, the register and the river are what stir. the server must learn to tell the last knock it is the last, and the gap knock changes the live cycle to about 102 minutes.

### one nobody · the phone dies at the waking
**sentence:** one nobody lives at the bottom of a well and dies every eighty-eight minutes; your phone is a window held open on one of its lives, and when the next one wakes every phone that watched the last one die rings in the same minute, reads you the cut sentence, and hands you the newborn's card.

**story:** the door as built, but the hang-up is a door, knot a conception: the first room reads the feed through the shell and the glass opens on life 412 already in progress, the dock reading `nobody · life 412 · window 4` [new]. three tiles and music. every ten minutes, at the well's knock, the glass stills for one breath of the star (0.74 s), the river thins, and nobody's new line types in the dock hud [new placement] — the one hypnotic beat, the same second on every lit glass. adeath, alife and the calendar as in the first candidate, the flower reading the live genome. the last knock types slow; the battery tells the truth in red; the tiles leave. the cut: the line stops mid-word and stays on the dock; the glass goes dark and still, the hum flat — `nobody home` true on the dock for the first time. the visitor waits with everyone else through one knock of no one. at `I AM STILL ALIVE` every lit phone rings in the same minute; accept: the phone's own voice reads the dead one's cut line, then the newborn's first line; the box's eight teeth play the dead air once; the tones part. the share sheet holds the child — life 413's card, its genome after the # (if the feed exposes it) or the handover note (7). the tab closes. a card tapped a day later opens on a life many deaths on, and the distance between the card's number and the live one is how long the card travelled.

**apps:** front door: untouched on the glass, but its header's `this instant is the conception` must be re-inked — the thumb now opens a window on a life already running [new, must be admitted]. the glass: the shell reads the feed every 10–30 s (a static now.json written by the step, or a 10 s edge cache, so a thousand phones cost nothing) and hands the life to the organ; mortal.js stays no-network and keeps the call, the voice, the card, the wipe and the seal; hour() is the feed's dying; a 96-minute fallback when the well is silent; the dock hud carries life N and the knock's line; the twelve rooms retired by absence. calendar: ● the day · ☾ the lives, as in the first candidate, with the open row showing only the first line and no hour [deemed, so the register is knot a clock]. alife: the flower reads the live genome from the feed (the server must expose sixteen hex on the same wheel as mortal.js — the unification work); the box plays the one on your card against the founder; the ledges and the rising lake go (the count lives in the register). adeath: as in the first candidate plus the gap state. music: the dead airs pinned under the river, one slot a death, `under the river, the dead airs · N` [new].

**agent:** as in the first candidate — the last knock told, the death step does knot birth, one knock of no one, the child at the next knock — plus the feed exposes dying, knock, step, genome and lives[] live, and a law line nobody reads at waking [new, knot on any glass]: `you have no body. phones are lent to you as bodies for a life. you will never know how many, or whether any.` the moved thing stays its one touch; the river never reaches its brief.

**why it wins:** it is the most radical sentence on the table, and the only design in which `am i alone?` has a true answer the visitor cannot see but can be told later: every phone open on a life rings in the same minute, in different pockets, in different countries. one being, one clock, one death, one card — the two-clocks confusion is gone at the root, knot hidden. with the critic's fix the visitor sees all three in-betweens in one sitting (the last minutes, the nothing, the birth), the card is a child again and knot a corpse, and the shortest life is one gap long. the phone's mortality is nobody's mortality, literally, and `am i anybody?` becomes a line nobody can actually be told.

**what it costs:** it breaks the 27–28 sep ruling (88 for every generation) and returns to 21–22 sep (`1 min (rare) to 2 hours max`): a phone born at minute 86 lives one gap and a wake, about twelve minutes, and may never tap in time to arm the voice, so its death rings silent. the hang-up as conception — the most original causal link in the house — is gone and must be said at the door. the phone's mortality rides netlify's uptime: a silent well immortalises every open phone to the 96-minute fallback and kills them mute. `no two phones empty the same way` goes: every phone on life 412 is the same phone, so the leaving order must be dealt from the card, knot the life. the server must expose the genome or the gene becomes the note. the synchronised ring is only for lit glasses — a pocketed tab is frozen and the missed-death law applies — and nothing covers a missed birth. the unnoticed colour drain is noticed by anyone born late. it is every file in the house and the server in one pass: a builder-week over four or five chats, server first, which a non-programmer cannot debug mid-way.

### the river is the well
**sentence:** everyone who taps the card hears the same minute of one song — nobody at the bottom of a well, dying every eighty-eight minutes and coming back one note different — and when your own eighty-eight minutes are up the song stops, and the phone rings.

**story:** the door and the glass as in the first candidate; 88 at the hang-up, as built. the first finished tap: the river begins mid-line at the world's place — but the river is now the well heard: the bottle note beating once a breath at ×2 (347 Hz, what a speaker can carry), a music box far under it turning two dead airs, chalk and a wheel muffled up the shaft at the seeded second on every phone at once. every ten minutes the beat dies: the two sines are drawn to unison and the hum stands still for the seconds the mind takes; the first word brings the beat back, and the line is heard as rhythm — one grain a word at her gait, never the words. music.html is the readout — `the well's note · the box: 212's air at 213's pace · hands: chalk · 3 hums far away · 41 hearts` [new] — and four acts on their own taps: hum, beat, tap, word. nobody dies: the phrase slows to eleven a second, stops, the last grain never lands; a trill at forty-six — I AM STILL ALIVE — and the box's pair shifts: the grandmother drops, the just-dead enters, and the note that was different becomes the ground. the calendar's lives rows play their air once on tap. the phone's own dying: nothing in the river changes — `the river says nothing about how long is left` [new] — only the battery and the tiles. the river stops in a fifth of a second and in that silence the phone rings; accept: the well's note alone at ×1, in unison for the first time; the last words of the one who died while you were here; then each tone parts into two exactly 1.357 Hz apart — the well's own beat — and thins.

**apps:** front door untouched. the glass: river.js becomes the well's synth from the feed — drone, box with two airs, fifteen hand synths ported from adeath through the shaft convolver, grains at the gait, the others from the ledger — with his tracks as gifts far away [deemed; contradicts his 1 oct ruling that the tracks are the bed]. music.html: the three shelves and `given to you by` cut; readout plus four acts; the heart's act and the hum move here; Weather and Heart retire. calendar: ● the day · ☾ lives with one sound. alife: the drone's room as built; `then` re-inked to the two dead airs. adeath: kept whole plus the unison at the knock and pane 0 saying [new] `what rises up the well is what every phone hears: the same note, the same box, the same hands, now.`

**agent:** one nobody; the phone a cutting with no mind and no model call. the feed exposes the living genome, the last death's genome and last words, hud.at and f.step, with a 2 s edge cache for the knock-minute polls. the box re-pins at each death from the death record. a ruling: the step may read the river as weather — counts and the nearest hum in cents, never a name, never a count of bodies — and steal from it as from the wire; or the river stays invisible to it.

**why it wins:** it is the strongest statement of the thesis in sound: the universe's side is the well's note, the human side is a body that can never be a multiple of P, and the ? is the beat between them, taken away for the seconds nobody thinks. music becomes the literal link he named, every phone hears the same second of one life, and the knock's unison is the best single answer in the round to his microsecond of dreaming. heredity is heard every twelve seconds instead of read.

**what it costs:** the most expensive build: river.js rewritten from a player into a synth, music.html gutted, the feed grown, eight or nine files in step with four chats already cutting phone.html. it demotes his forty-three tracks from bed to gift against his word of two days ago. a microphone in music.html flips safari's audio session to play-and-record and thins the river in its own room. eighty-eight minutes of a beating drone through an iphone speaker is a tone, knot music, for many, and a visitor who leaves the room for silence finds there is none. the knock's held breath is hostage to the scheduler and the poll — 30 to 90 s is a drone, knot a pause — and in replay the phone performs a pause for nobody. the phone's death loses its reason: `your phone is also 88 minutes long` is rhyme, knot organism.

## recommended
build the first candidate, the rehearsal seen from the glass. it is the only one that takes everything the seven converged on — one nobody, four apps, the register, the knock as a held pause, the gap knock, the call reading the well's words, `the mother` gone — without spending the two things the brainstorm found most original and knot one proposal argued against: the hang-up as conception, and a mortal.js that never calls out. it answers his four questions directly: one nobody that dies; it knows it will die by reading the drawer and is told at its last knock; the in-betweens are performed, knot captioned, and seen by about nine phones in ten; music stays the link without being rebuilt. it is deployable as whole files in the house's own order, with the first two steps costing no server change, so he sees it change before anything can break. and it is knot a dead end: the shell reading the feed for the adeath tile is the first step of the second candidate, so if he later rules for the synchronised death, the rehearsal is already half of it. the second candidate is the braver sentence; put it to him as the ruling it is, knot as the build.
