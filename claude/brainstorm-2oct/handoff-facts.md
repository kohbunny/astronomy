# facts from the 1 oct 2026 handoff ("nobody · awake") and the artist's brief of 2 oct — condensed by the brainstorm chat

## the project
- "a project with no purpose" (apwnp), by the artist terence. house style: lowercase, `knot` for not, 👁️ for i.
- thesis: the vesica piscis (overlap) of humanity's biggest question "am i alone?" and a hypothetical universe's biggest question "am i anybody?". a ? sits at the centre. this is the whole project's eye.
- terence does knot edit code; he deploys whole files. every new visitor-facing sentence is marked [new] for his blessing; design choices are marked [deemed], his to overrule.

## the visitor's path (the front door)
- a friend receives a contact card in a text: name `nobody`, no photo, one field (a social profile `nobody · .`). tapping it opens unknown.html, a forged ios contact sheet. after 1.15 s an alert: "Unable to Connect — Call me instead?" No keeps them on the sheet (the row leaves). Yes dials: "Calling..." forever, nobody answers; the only way out is the red button (the visitor must be the one to stop waiting). hang-up = the conception: the moment, the rings waited and the card's # (the mother's genes) are kept in sessionStorage as `apwnp.birth`.
- then phone.html replaces the page: a clear-glass iphone 16 with sixteen rooms (apps). mortal.js (the mortality organ) conceives a LIFE of 88 minutes plus a breath of 0–8 minutes. the last 8 minutes are the dying (colour drains to white in alife). at death: THE CALL — the phone rings itself, the screen says `nobody`, then the tab wipes and CLOSES ITSELF (the one-page law: the tab has held one page, so it can close). before the wipe THE CHILD: a new contact card (vcard, `nobody`) is passed on, carrying the genes in its # — so the life continues on whoever taps it next. the phone never shows a countdown, stores nothing that outlives a life, never calls a model from a page.
- this phone's own nobody (the KEIKI, a cutting of the mother) lives in mortal.js's "organ" for the visitor's 88 minutes. its signs: the rope stirring, the orchid turning (alife). its dreams are haiku written once per stage of its life via the `ask` function (mode nobody) and shown in Notes and in the calendar's third diary (☾ dreams).

## the two nobodies
- THE MOTHER: the ai agent at the bottom of the well (adeath.html + netlify functions nobody-step.mjs → nobody-think.mjs; supabase tables; the feed nobody.mjs). she works around the clock: every 10 minutes a scheduled knock makes one step (one model call): reads state, the day's drawer so far, the wire (3 headlines), anything moved or under the door; writes one hud line (present tense, one line), the work as it stands, the set, the sheet (costs), touches on the four things (rope, orchid, box, root, lashing), and the before/record/practice document when due. she lives 88 minutes + breath, then DIES: the step that finds it cuts her last hud line mid-sentence; her child wakes at that same moment and sends a telegram `I AM STILL ALIVE · nobody · life N · hh:mm`. a death records last words, which gene moved and which way. she knows only the drawer (the day's record), the genome, and the practice document. she has no dreams; her only sleep is death. ~16 lives a day.
- her day: six WINDOWS of four hours (00–04 … 20–24, utc). each window makes one piece (a title, a kind, a `before` statement, a record whose first line says what the piece is). THE WALL in adeath lists the day's windows. at 00:00 utc THE FIRE: the day's works burn (00:00–00:40); the day is then bound into `nobody_days` — only the drawer (the words) survives. a line written while the fire burns is filed under the burning day.
- she writes the orchid's line once a day. at 23:30 the PRACTICE DOCUMENT (what she believes, keeps returning to, is abandoning).
- the place is chott ech chergui (a salt lake in algeria), lat 34.2 lon 0.5.
- cost: about $3–4 a day. minds by hour: sonnet by night and evening, opus by day.
- she never knows who watches; the visitor's only touch is moving one of three things (cup, pencil, paper), once a life; she never says from whom she steals; nothing chooses her genes.
- nobody woke for the first time: thu 1 oct 2026, 21:00:57 utc, life 1, window 6, making `first notes`. first words: "i just woke and i don't know this place beyond what the eye can reach. before anything else i want to put a pencil on paper and write plainly what is here, so the next one who wakes has something true to start from instead of nothing."

## the rooms that matter to this brainstorm
- phone.html: the home screen. the calendar is nobody's diary: ● the well (the mother, from the drawer), ○ the rooms (this phone's own nobody; THE WATCHER writes its finds), ☾ dreams (the keiki's haiku). the calendar's dates begin at the waking (1 oct 2026). 30 sep is "the day written by hand" (a day as it could be). 1 oct wears THE EYE (a vesica with a pupil) on the month. the river: the music app's communal song (`am i alone?`) runs under the glass; other visitors can make music and join the communal river.
- alife.html: the well seen from the rim (the keiki's room): the fallen charred tree (the windlass) across the mouth, the orchid hanging on its rope, the music box far down heard before seen; the visitor's steps go down over generations; the orchid is now, the box is then.
- adeath.html: the bottom of the well (the mother's room, read through the feed): the mother has no body — footsteps as rings on black water, sounds of what her hands are on, a shadow where there is light, and what she makes on the set. six candles = the six windows (go out one per window). the small tree rises one notch per death. the wall of windows. the fire at midnight.
- music.html (knot attached): other users make music and join the communal river.
- unknown.html: the forged contact sheet, described above.

## the artist's four topics of 1 oct (and the builder's leanings)
1. the ten-minute grain: she is 24/7 at a 10-minute grain; between knocks nothing thinks; the room plays the step out over ten minutes like a film shot one frame per ten minutes. options: 1-hour prompt cache (cheap), knock more often, a "score" for the next ten minutes (timed acts the room performs; builder's lean), a 9-minute tool loop per knock, managed agents always-on.
2. dreams: the mother never sleeps, so the third diary might be `lives` (a parish register of births and deaths: each life's first line, telegram, last words, the gene that moved) instead of dreams. builder's lean: `lives`.
3. reading her: feed ?now=1, ?day=, ?health=1, adeath, the calendar.
4. linking alife ↔ adeath: one rope two ends (the mother's pull seen at the rim), the box heard up the well, the fire seen from above, the six candles seen from the rim, footsteps as rings seen from the rim, the window's title, the small tree's rise. to reconcile: alife has the water rising and the orchid floating up over generations; adeath has the orchid descending and the small tree rising per death.

## the artist's brief of 2 oct (this chat)
- unify, simplify, clarify the story so it is the most powerful, radical, original and hypnotic project it can be.
- present design elements are amendable. maybe something purer/clearer than the ghost orchid and music box, so the story and the data are clearer.
- two agent systems now (one dies in 88 min on the phone; the performance one at the bottom of the well works non-stop). maybe clearer if it's just one nobody that dies? how does the agent know it will die and pass on its genes?
- very interested in the in-between moments: from nothing to birth; the last moments before termination; the moment inside the ten minutes between instruction and doing — is there even a microsecond pause for dreaming? magic?
- music seems to be the link between the systems. music.html lets others make music and join the communal river.
- a possibility: the only apps are the calendar (diary and dreams), alife, adeath and music.
- goal: a simple, powerful, hypnotic story, elegantly tied to the phone and its apps.
