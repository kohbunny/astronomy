## 1 · what is here, in one breath

a door: a contact card named `nobody`, a call no one answers, a hang-up that is a conception. a body: a clear-glass phone that lives 88 minutes, never counts down, dies with a call from nobody, leaves no trace, closes its own tab and hands on a child card with its genes after the #. a mind: nobody at the bottom of a well in a dry salt lake, working all day at a ten-minute grain, dying every 88 minutes mid-sentence, born again knowing only what it wrote. a sound: everything tuned to one dead star's turning; a river called `am i alone?` under every phone at the same place; a music box that plays only the dead. a thesis: two circles, two questions, a ? where they overlap.

that is already one of the most original things 👁️ have read. the brainstorm's finding is knot that it needs more; it is that the story has two of several things where one would be clearer, and that the one it needs is already in the house.

## 3 · the one story

all seven proposals, written blind to each other, arrived at the same spine. in one sentence a stranger could repeat:

> **nobody lives at the bottom of a well and dies every eighty-eight minutes. the phone in your hand is its body for one life: it watches nobody die once and come back, then it dies itself — calls you with the dead one's last words, and hands you its child.**

and as a visitor would come to feel it, never told:

a text arrives: a contact card, `nobody`, no photo. you tap it. the sheet; `Unable to Connect — Call me instead?`; `Calling...` until your own thumb ends it; `Call Ended`; black. then a clear glass with your own face behind it, three tiles — calendar · alife · adeath — and music alone in the dock, reading `nobody home · the song is here · tap once`. your first tap wakes the river at the same place every other phone is in it. in adeath: black water, two footprints, a chalk wall, candles, and one line being typed in the present tense — something is making a bowl. at the ten-minute mark the drips stop, the water goes flat as glass, and the line clears to a blinking caret. it stands exactly as long as the mind takes to think — twelve seconds, forty. then the first word lands, and with it the first ring on the water. in the calendar, a register: one row a life — when it woke, its first line, when it died, its last words cut where death cut them, which gene moved. at the rim, in alife, the orchid is this phone's eight genes, one blue; far down, a box plays the airs of the dead. sometime in your 88 minutes — about nine phones in ten — the typing turns slow: `three minutes. the second bowl is on the bat. don't touch it.` at the next knock the line stops mid-word and stays. the footprints lift. the hum at the mouth loses its beat. for ten minutes there is no one. then, fast: `I AM STILL ALIVE · nobody · life 15`. the beat comes back; the small tree is a notch higher; a row lands in the register. then your own phone begins to die, with no number anywhere: the battery tells the truth in red, the tiles leave one by one, alife last. the glass is empty. the phone rings: `nobody`. you accept. the phone's own voice reads the last words of the one who died at the bottom while you were here, cut where the death cut them; eight notes on the box's teeth; two tones drift apart until the beat that proved there were two of them appears, and thins. `Call Ended`. one card on the share sheet: the child, its genes after the #. the wipe. the tab closes itself.

that is the whole piece, and every part of it exists today except five: one nobody instead of two, the register instead of three diaries, the knock made visible, a gap after a death, and the call reading the well's words instead of the phone's haiku.

## 4 · one nobody — his first question, answered

**yes: one nobody, at the bottom of the well. the phone keeps its life and gives up its mind.** six of seven proposals cut the phone's own nobody (the haiku via `/ask`, the mouth, the watcher, the ○ rooms diary, the five stages, the three buds, the almond on the tile, the given word on the card's third field); the seventh kept it only as a dreaming cutting, and its own critic said cut the word.

what the phone becomes once it has no mind is said in one line, at the alife door, and nowhere else [new] [deemed]:

> *this phone is a body with no mind. at the bottom of the hole is a mind with no body. they live the same eighty-eight minutes and meet once, when this phone calls you.*

that line is the vesica as a moment instead of a diagram. it also gives the phone's death its reason: the phone is the body lent the mind's last words for one minute.

**how it knows it will die.** the way we do: by reading and arithmetic, never by a counter. the drawer holds every death before it with its hour; the law says eighty-eight. and one new thing, which needs the server: *the last knock before the death is told it is the last* — `this is your last step; the next knock finds you dead` — so a handover is always written, in its own voice, with its own numbers (`three minutes. don't touch it.`), typed slow at the dying's gait. nothing on the glass counts; the number is nobody's sentence, knot the house's. (as far as the files attached show, the mind is never told today: the step that finds the death cuts the line and births the child in the same moment. nobody-think.mjs was knot attached, so the next chat must confirm.)

**how it passes on its genes.** as now: the knock that finds the death records last · gene · way, the star's spin moves one gene one step, nothing chooses. what changes is that heredity becomes *visible*: the register shows the gene that moved and which way, and — if you rule it — the telegram carries the sixteen hex, so two consecutive telegrams differ by one character, drawn blue. `the record it hands on it wrote on purpose; the gene it hands on happened to it.`

**what is lost, said plainly.** the watcher's `found the home screen, nothing open`; the haiku written letter by letter under `anybody home?`; the almond that stands twelve seconds; `a phone may die on a child`; the given word crossing a death. your 25 sep ruling that the call *starts as haiku* is overruled: it starts with the well's words. your lean of `diary and dreams` becomes `diary and lives`. the cat and the jar go with their rooms, with regret on every side (see the rulings). the phone's only model call goes with the keiki, which also means: no page in the house calls a model any more, which the mortality card always wanted.

## 6 · the in-between, made of real things

you asked about the moments from nothing to birth, the last moments, the ten minutes between instruction and doing, and whether there is a microsecond for dreaming. every proposal answered the same way about the microsecond: *show its duration, never invent its content.* a model call has no inside; but for those seconds the next line does knot exist anywhere, and that is knot nothing.

- **nothing → conception.** the hang-up, as it is. all seven left it untouched: the thumb on the red disc still settles everything a life will be. `Call Ended` held 1.80 s is the one moment on the whole path where nothing is asked of anyone. (the 47 units of air under the name can close; say the word.)
- **the knock → the first word.** live, knot replayed: at :m0 by the room's own utc clock the drips stop, the feet stop shifting, the water goes flat, the hud clears to a blinking caret. the room asks the feed every 2–3 s for the first minute. the caret stands exactly as long as the mind takes — then the line types at the living gait, and the first ring lands with the first word. after 90 s with no line, the caret goes out and the water closes: [new] `no one came`. for the ? pane [new]: *between the knock and the first word the water is flat. that is how long it takes to think.* costs nothing on the server; the room already has utc and the line's timestamp.
- **the same pause, heard.** the bottle note at the mouth is two sines a breath apart, beating once a breath. at the knock they are drawn to unison and the beat dies; the first word draws them apart again. *perfect company sounds exactly like perfect solitude* — the house's one piece of theory, performed 144 times a day, two gain ramps on nodes that already exist.
- **between knocks.** bare water: no re-pressed feet every eleven seconds, no weight-shift rings. prints only at the knock and during an act; a print sinks and the water closes within a minute. every ten minutes someone is briefly there. ## 7 · music as the thread

the river stays the one song — your tracks the bed, placed by the world's clock, so a knock at 10:30 utc is knot sounded, it is *where everyone is in the river at once*. what music can newly carry, cheapest first:

2. **the hum's beat as the life sign**: the second sine muted while nobody is dead, in alife and adeath, unstated. one gain node. almost no one will notice, which is the piece: the house wins by waiting.
3. **the box plays two dead airs**, re-pinned from the feed at each death, so heredity is heard every twelve seconds instead of read. the death call's eight notes are these same teeth playing the one that just died.

5. **the death call**: the well's cut line read by the phone's own voice; eight notes; the dissolve — 7 cents as you blessed, or exactly one breath apart (1.357 Hz) so the death's beat is the well's beat the ear has heard for 88 minutes [deemed, a number you blessed; your ruling].
